# Visual provenance: generative-model module

## Scope

The two SVG figures in this folder are original, editable, conceptual teaching schematics created for this repository. They are not derived from a measured data set, a simulation output, or a third-party figure.

## Figure jobs and evidence boundary

| File | Figure job | Evidence status | Visual meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `generative_task_map.svg` | Distinguish reconstruction, generation, and inverse design before model selection. | Conceptual | Arrows show information flow; boxes name task inputs/outputs and evaluation needs. |
| `generative_evidence_pipeline.svg` | Show the checks between generated output and a bounded engineering claim. | Conceptual | Arrows show a required evidence sequence, not time, physical transport, or a validated workflow. |

## Construction and checks

- Native SVG with selectable text, original geometry, and no external images, logos, or third-party visual material.
- Blue/purple/green fills differentiate task roles but labels and box positions retain meaning in grayscale.
- Diagrams are not to scale. They introduce no measured values, performance metrics, or physical mechanisms beyond the module's stated conceptual scope.
