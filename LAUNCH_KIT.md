## Launch post draft

I have renamed and expanded our open **Machine Learning for Engineers** curriculum. It is designed around physical engineering questions—not just model execution.

Students work from an engineering question to data and assumptions, a baseline, meaningful held-out evaluation, failure analysis, and an interpretation tied to a decision. The current release includes six modules, from regression and vision to a heat-conduction physics-informed neural-network module, plus AI-use documentation and instructor adoption materials.

The repository is open under BSD 3-Clause and is intended for instructors who want to adapt one module or an entire project-based course. Feedback, issues, and carefully scoped contributions are welcome.

Link: `https://github.com/hanhuark/machine-learning-for-engineers`
