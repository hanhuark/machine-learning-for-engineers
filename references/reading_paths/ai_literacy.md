# Reading path: AI literacy and accountable use

1. Long and Magerko: core AI-literacy competencies.
2. UNESCO and institutional teaching guidance: policy and educational context.
3. NIST AI RMF: risk, documentation, and accountability vocabulary.
4. Engineering GenAI reviews: current opportunities, risks, and evidence gaps.
5. [AI agent harnesses](../../course_content/ix_ai_agent_harnesses/) and the [AI-use record](../../teaching_resources/AI_USE_TEMPLATE.md).

The intended outcome is engineering responsibility: students should know what tool was used, what it was allowed to access, how its output was checked, and what evidence remains independent of the tool.
