# Reading path: physical and embodied AI

1. Brunton and Kutz: dynamics, reduced-order models, and control.
2. Sharma et al.: physics-informed modeling and validation limits.
3. Robotics/PBL reviews: use physical systems as evidence of integration, not as spectacle.
4. [Hardware and deployment pathway](../../hardware_and_deployment/).
5. [Reinforcement-learning module](../../course_content/v_reinforcement_learning/) and [PINN module](../../course_content/viii_physics_informed_neural_networks/).

A hardware demonstration should require a system boundary, sensing/time-base definition, safety constraints, baseline controller, perturbation test, and individual explanation.
