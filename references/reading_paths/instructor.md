# Reading path: instructor or adopter

1. Singelmann and Covarrubias: structure around data, task, algorithm, and assessment.
2. The Carpentries lesson-design guide: define outcomes before writing content.
3. The project/challenge-based engineering reviews: choose an authentic task with visible evidence of learning.
4. [Module development standard](../../teaching_resources/MODULE_DEVELOPMENT_STANDARD.md).
5. [Adoption guide](../../ADOPTION_GUIDE.md) and [assessment policy](../../teaching_resources/ASSESSMENT_AND_AI_POLICY.md).

Adopt one validated module before attempting a whole-course conversion. Preserve the shared evidence standard even when the disciplinary context changes.
